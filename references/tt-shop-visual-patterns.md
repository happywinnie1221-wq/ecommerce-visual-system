# TikTok Shop Visual Patterns

## Nine-Image Grid

Use this structure for overseas TikTok Shop, Amazon-style PDPs, Shopify product galleries, and listing uploads.

1. Main white-background product image: straight-on hero, clean, no props.
2. Angle hero: 30-45 degree view, shows depth and side details.
3. On-product artwork or design detail: make clear the pattern is on the product surface.
4. Material macro: texture, edge, button, seam, closure, lens rim, grip, or finish.
5. Lifestyle usage: hand, desk, vanity, travel, gym, beach, or daily scene depending on product.
6. Packaging or bundle: box, pouch, accessories, what-you-get trust image.
7. Functional detail: protection, fit, size, ports, closure, strap, surface, anti-slip, or capacity.
8. Front/back or variant pair: show fit, scale, color family, or device compatibility.
9. Shoppable photo scene: social-commerce still with soft lifestyle styling and product as hero.

Marketplace cleanliness rules:

- No promotional overlay text.
- No badges, borders, watermarks, discount tags, or fake UI.
- Main image should be white or very light and product-forward.
- Additional images can include realistic context but should remain low clutter.

## Connected Long-Strip Narrative

Use this structure for TikTok Photo Mode, Xiaohongshu-style carousels, landing-page strips, and detail-page story images.

1. Hook panel: product enters the frame; strong first glance, no heavy copy unless user requests copy.
2. Product reveal: clean hero product, brand identity visible.
3. Design story: origin of pattern, material, color, motif, or inspiration.
4. Material proof: macro texture and construction detail.
5. In-use scene: real scale and daily context.
6. Trust/bundle: packaging, included items, care detail, or giftability.
7. Final hold: clean product close, palette closure, brand memory.

Continuity rules:

- Keep the same product shape, colorway, logo position, and material across panels.
- Use a shared palette and consistent lighting direction.
- Make each panel readable alone, but visually linked to the previous and next panel.
- If composing into one long image, keep each panel's important product content away from crop boundaries.
